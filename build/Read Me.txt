BenchBook for Windows
=====================

A private reference library for scale modellers.

To run it
---------

1. Move this BenchBook folder wherever you like, e.g. into Documents.
2. Open it and double-click BenchBook.exe.
3. The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". This download
   isn't signed with a certificate. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

To keep it handy, right-click BenchBook.exe and choose "Pin to Start" or
"Pin to taskbar".

Your library
------------

Your library is kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BenchBook, not in this folder. Deleting
or replacing this folder never deletes your library.

To update, use Check Now in Settings, or let BenchBook check once a day. It
shows what's new first, then replaces this folder with the new version and
opens again. You can also close BenchBook, delete this folder and unzip the new
version in its place.

BenchBook stays on your computer. It has no accounts and sends nothing about
you anywhere. It only goes online to fetch the title and picture for links you
save, and to check for updates if you let it. Both can be turned off in
Settings.
